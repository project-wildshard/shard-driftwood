# The Drowned Captain as rows

ONE-FORMAT OF22c2. The Captain was the engine's reserved body under the
native wake shell. Now he is a declared spawn at the pool
(`driftwood.captain`) with a `boss` encounter (`boss.captain`, start
`spawned`, capExempt, arena 22, phases 1 / 0.66 / 0.33), and his
decision row binds that encounter. The chapter's wake row already names
his spawn, so engine (OF22c1) summons the fight on the altar's cue.

Format 0.55.2 lets a boss encounter's declared spawn hold the decision
row's public fields, as the runtime boss spawn did, so the runtime boss
spawn goes. His species row has no abilities and no body look yet: his
blows are the decision row's `actors/strikes`, and the page draws him
from its packed looks by kind, like the roster (OF22b1's look half).

The shrine journey changed in three ways. It checks he is the declared
spawn. It counts only living captains, because his dormant body waits in
the world until the cue. The fight helper now dodges on the strike
runner's windup, which is what the page draws for a decision-driven
body; his body state stays idle. It also waits one step for the kill
flag, which the encounter files after the fall.
